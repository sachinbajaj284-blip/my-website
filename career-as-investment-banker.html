How to Become an Investment Banker in India

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated October 2026 · 9 min read

Investment banking is high finance at high intensity — raising capital, advising on mergers and deals, and valuing companies. The pay is among the best in the country and the hours are among the hardest; it rewards ambition, stamina and precision in equal measure.

💼 What an investment banker actually does

An investment banker helps companies and governments raise money and do deals — IPOs and share sales, bond issues, mergers and acquisitions, and restructuring. The work is financial modelling, valuation, pitch books and client advice, under real deadlines. It splits into areas like M&A advisory, capital markets and sales & trading, and the elite "bulge-bracket" global banks differ sharply from domestic firms in both pay and intensity.

🧭 The path after Class 10

Stream is not an absolute barrier, but Commerce with Mathematics is the natural and strongest base — and Maths genuinely matters for the finance that follows. Economics and strong quantitative ability are prized throughout this career.

🎓 The path after Class 12

  • Top-college UG (B.Com / BBA / Economics) — from target colleges like SRCC, St. Stephen’s, NMIMS or similar (via CUET / institute tests); these feed analyst roles directly.
  • MBA in Finance — from an IIM, ISB or top B-school (via CAT / GMAT); the most common route into front-office investment banking.
  • CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) — a globally respected finance qualification, strong for research and valuation roles.
  • CA → finance — chartered accountants with strong finance skills also move into banking and deal roles.

🛠️ Core skills to build

  • Financial modelling & valuation — the technical core — Excel, DCF, comparables, deal maths.
  • Stamina and composure — genuinely long hours under pressure; this is non-negotiable.
  • Communication — turning analysis into a pitch a client and a committee will buy.
  • Attention to detail — in a pitch book or a model, small errors are expensive.

🧩 Is this career right for you?

Investment banking suits strong Enterprising interests (ambition, deals, persuasion) combined with Conventional (precision with numbers and process) and Investigative (analysis). If you are driven, resilient under pressure and genuinely energised by high-stakes finance — and clear-eyed about the hours — it is a demanding but exceptionally rewarding fit.

Key takeaways
  • Commerce-with-Maths or Economics is the strongest base; a top MBA or CFA is the usual route in.
  • Target-college UG can feed analyst roles directly; CA is another route into deal work.
  • Pay is among the best in India — and the hours are among the hardest. Go in clear-eyed.
  • Best-fit interests: Enterprising + Conventional + Investigative. You are ambitious, precise and resilient.
