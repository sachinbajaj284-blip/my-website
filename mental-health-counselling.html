Start with the check, not the booking

Most people who need support don't arrive looking for a counsellor. They arrive trying to work out whether what they are feeling is normal, or a phase, or something worth taking seriously. That question deserves an answer before anyone asks you to pay for anything.

So start here. The self-checks below use the same brief reflection scales clinicians use as conversation starters — PHQ-4, GAD-7, the Rosenberg self-esteem scale. They take two to four minutes, they are free, and your answers are never stored, sent or seen by us. You get a band and a plain-English explanation of what it suggests. What you do next is entirely your call.

Confidential means confidential

The single most common reason people in India don't book a session isn't the price. It's the fear that someone will find out — that a parent will ask what the payment was for, that a college will note it somewhere, that an employer will treat it as a liability.

So, plainly:

  • Nothing is shared with your parents, school, college or employer. No report, no summary, no confirmation that you attended.
  • You can book using a first name only. You don't owe us your full name, your institution, or your reason in advance.
  • The self-checks store nothing. Your answers live in your browser for the length of the check and are gone when you close it.
  • Sessions are online by default, so there is no waiting room and nobody to run into.
There is one limit on confidentiality, and it is the same one every counsellor and clinician works under: if there is a serious risk to your life or to someone else's, your counsellor will talk with you about involving someone who can keep you safe. You will not be surprised by it, and it will be discussed with you, not around you.

What this is — and what it isn't

Being straight about scope matters more here than in any other kind of service, so here is the honest version.

This is non-diagnostic counselling support: structured, confidential conversations about what you're dealing with, what's keeping it going, and what would practically help. It's useful for the very large space between "I'm fine" and "I need psychiatric care" — which is where most people actually are.

This isn't a diagnosis, a prescription, or an emergency service. We don't label conditions and we don't prescribe medication. If what you describe would be better served by a psychiatrist or intensive care, your counsellor will tell you that in the first session rather than sell you a package — and will help you work out where to go.

Frequently asked questions

Will my parents, school or employer be told?

No. Sessions are confidential — nothing is reported to your parents, school, college or employer, and no record is shared with anyone. You can book using a first name only. The one exception is the standard limit above: a serious risk to your life or someone else's.

How much does online mental health counselling cost in India?

A Lume Live session is ₹499 for 45 minutes, and your first is ₹249 with code FIRST50. Typical private counselling runs ₹1,500–₹3,000 per session.

Is the free self-check a diagnosis?

No. The checks use well-known reflection scales such as PHQ-4 and GAD-7 and give you a band to think about, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified clinician can diagnose a condition, and that takes a proper assessment rather than a questionnaire. Your answers aren't stored.

I'm under 18. Can I book?

A parent or guardian needs to consent to a paid session if you're under 18. The free self-checks are open to anyone — and if talking to your parents about this feels hard, that's itself something worth planning with support. Tele-MANAS on 14416 is free, confidential and available to under-18s without parental consent.

Is counselling the same as therapy or psychiatry?

No. This is non-diagnostic counselling support. We don't diagnose conditions and we don't prescribe medication. If what you describe needs psychiatric care, your counsellor will say so and help you find it.

What actually happens in a first session?

You talk, and someone qualified listens properly. No form, no test. Most of it is spent understanding what's been going on, for how long, and what you've already tried. It ends with one or two concrete things to try and an honest view on whether more sessions would help.

Do you offer sessions in Hindi?

Yes — Hindi or English, whichever you actually think in. For most people that matters more than they expect.
