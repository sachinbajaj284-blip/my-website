What trauma counselling support can help with

You don’t need a diagnosis, and you don’t have to recount everything to get support. These are the things people bring:

🌊
Flashbacks & intrusions

Memories, images or feelings that arrive uninvited, as though it’s happening now rather than then.

😰
Hypervigilance

Always on guard, easily startled, scanning for danger, unable to fully relax even when safe.

🌙
Nightmares & broken sleep

The day’s guard dropping at night, so what you keep at bay returns while you sleep.

🧊
Numbness & disconnection

Feeling flat, far away or cut off from people and from yourself — the mind’s way of coping.

🚪
Avoidance

Steering around reminders — places, people, conversations — with life narrowing to stay away from them.

💔
Guilt, shame & anger

Blaming yourself, “why me”, or a short fuse that doesn’t feel like you — all common after trauma.

Frequently asked questions

Can counselling help with trauma and PTSD?

Yes, and support helps many people a great deal. It’s worth being clear, though: PTSD has specialist, evidence-based treatments — trauma-focused CBT and EMDR — and more severe or complex trauma is best worked with by a trauma specialist, sometimes alongside a psychiatrist. We offer compassionate, non-diagnostic support, and where specialist care is the right step, we’ll say so and help you find it.

Do I have to describe what happened?

No. You never have to recount the trauma in detail to be supported, and certainly not before you’re ready — pushing into it too fast can do harm. You set the pace entirely; a great deal of useful work happens without reliving the event.

Is the self-check a diagnosis?

No. It’s a brief reflection tool, not a diagnosis of PTSD, and your answers aren’t stored. Only a qualified professional can diagnose; if what you describe needs specialist trauma care, your counsellor will tell you and help you find it.

Will anyone find out?

No. Nothing is shared with your parents, school, college or employer — no report, no summary, not even confirmation that you attended. A first name is enough to book.

What if I’m in crisis?

Counselling isn’t a crisis service. If you’re in immediate distress or unsafe, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 now — free, confidential and available 24×7.
