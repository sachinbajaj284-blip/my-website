FAQ

Career counselling kis age/class ke liye sahi hai?

Class 9 se 12 tak ke students ke liye stream aur subject decisions mein, aur college students/young adults ke liye career direction mein sabse zyada helpful hai. Working professionals ke liye alag Lume Lens profile hai.

₹49 introductory session mein kya hota hai?

Ye 45-minute ki 1:1 conversation hai (regular ₹499). Hum aapki current concern samajhte hain — stream choice, career direction ya emotional stress — aur agla step plan karte hain. Koi bhi formal assessment pehle explain kiya jata hai.

Kya online counselling effective hai?

Haan. Sabhi sessions secure video call (Google Meet/Zoom) par hote hain — ghar baithe. Research dikhati hai ki tele-counselling in-person jaisa hi effective hai. Humne 500+ students ko online guide kiya hai.

Kya meri baatein confidential rehti hain?

Bilkul. 100% confidentiality humari highest priority hai. Aapki personal baatein sirf aapke aur counsellor ke beech rehti hain — parents, school ya kisi third party ke saath bina consent share nahi hoti.
