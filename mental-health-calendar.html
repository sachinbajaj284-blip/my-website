Talking about mental health gets easier when there is a reason to start the conversation. This calendar gathers the awareness days and pressure-points of the Indian student year — board exams, results season, World Mental Health Day, Suicide Prevention Day and more — and pairs each with a short, plain-language note you can actually use. The entry for today, or the current month, is highlighted at the top.

Nothing here is a diagnosis, and no single day defines how you feel. These notes are for reflection and for talking things through — with a friend, a family member, or a counsellor.

If this is an emergency: counselling isn't a crisis service. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, or having thoughts of self-harm, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 now. It's free, it's 24×7, and it's run by the Government of India. Or go straight to the nearest hospital emergency department. Please don't sit and wait for a session.

How to use this calendar

Share a relevant day with your class, your team or your family as a gentle way to open up a conversation. Use the linked checks and guides when a date speaks to something you are going through. And remember that awareness days are prompts, not deadlines — support is available on every other day of the year too.
