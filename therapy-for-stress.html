What counselling for stress & burnout can help with

You don’t need to be at breaking point to book. These are the things people bring when stress has stopped easing on its own:

🥝
Exhaustion that rest won’t fix

Running on empty, waking already tired, weekends that no longer recharge you.

📉
Dread & detachment

The Sunday-evening sinking feeling, going numb or cynical about work, pulling back from people.

🏗️
Overwhelm & overload

Too much on, too little control, never switching off, always reachable — and no idea where to start cutting.

🚫
Boundaries that won’t hold

Saying yes when you mean no, guilt about resting, work bleeding into every evening.

💤
The physical toll

Disturbed sleep, headaches, getting ill often, irritability, trouble concentrating.

🧐
Feeling ineffective

A sense that nothing you do counts any more, dropping confidence, more mistakes — effort that stopped feeling worth it.

Frequently asked questions

Can counselling actually help with burnout?

Yes. A counsellor can help you see the pattern clearly, separate what you can control from what you can’t, build boundaries that hold, reconnect with what matters, and think through bigger decisions about work or study — all without the pressure deepening while you do it.

Is the burnout check a diagnosis?

No. It’s a brief reflection tool that gives you a quick read on how heavy the load has become. It isn’t a diagnosis — burnout isn’t a formal medical diagnosis on its own — and your answers aren’t stored or sent anywhere.

Isn’t burnout just a sign I can’t handle pressure?

No — burnout most often hits committed, capable people precisely because they take on load and keep going. It’s a response to prolonged overload, not a measure of weakness, and it responds well to the right support.

Will my employer or anyone else find out?

No. Nothing is shared with your employer, parents, school or college — no report, no summary, not even confirmation that you attended. A first name is enough to book.
