What counselling for low mood can help with in Gurugram

You don’t need a diagnosis or a reason that looks big enough from outside. These are the things people bring when low mood has been weighing on them:

🌁
Loss of interest

Things you used to enjoy going flat — hobbies, people, plans. Often the earliest and most honest sign.

😶
Exhaustion & sleep

Tiredness sleep doesn’t fix, mornings getting harder, everything taking more effort than it should.

🧠
The harsh inner voice

Guilt, feeling like a burden, a running self-criticism you’d never accept aimed at anyone else.

😓
Withdrawing

Pulling back from friends and family without quite deciding to, and the loneliness that follows.

🌑
Numbness, not just sadness

Feeling flat or empty rather than tearful — the version of low mood that’s easiest to miss and dismiss.

🫀
Feeling stuck

Drift after a setback, a loss or a change — where low mood and feeling directionless feed each other.

Frequently asked questions

Will my employer know I booked a session?

No. This is completely independent of any corporate wellness programme — your company isn’t involved, isn’t billed, and isn’t told. You can book using a first name only.

Can I do a session outside office hours?

Yes. You pick your own slot from the live calendar, and evening and weekend times are available. Sessions are online, so there’s no commute added to your day.

My problem is genuinely my workload. Can counselling fix that?

It can’t change your workload, and it would be dishonest to claim otherwise. What it can help with is the part you do control — boundaries, what you want to ask for, how you’re reading the situation, and whether staying is the right call. Sometimes the honest conclusion is that the job is the problem.

I am a parent in Gurugram worried about my teenager. Where do I start?

A parent session is a reasonable first step, and it doesn’t require your child to agree to anything. It is often more useful than pushing a reluctant teenager into a room. Our guide for parents covers the signs worth taking seriously.

Can counselling help with depression?

Often, yes — especially for mild to moderate low mood, where counselling, support and small changes make a real difference. More severe depression sometimes needs medication alongside, and a counsellor will tell you honestly.

I feel numb rather than sad. Is that still worth talking about?

Yes. Loss of feeling — not enjoying anything, feeling flat or empty — is a core feature of depression and is often missed. It’s very much worth talking through.
