How to Become a Fashion Designer in India

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

Fashion design is creativity made wearable — and a real industry with craft, business and deadlines behind the glamour. Any stream can enter; what you need is a strong portfolio and a point of view.

What a fashion designer actually does

A fashion designer conceives and creates clothing and accessories — researching trends, sketching, selecting fabrics, and overseeing garments from concept to finished piece. The field spans apparel, textiles, accessories, costume and increasingly sustainable and digital fashion. Beyond designing, careers include styling, merchandising, production and launching your own label, which is as much business as art.

The path after Class 10

Stream is not a barrier — any stream can lead to fashion design. What matters far more is building creative skill and a portfolio: sketching, an eye for colour and form, and curiosity about how clothes are made. Start a sketchbook early.

The path after Class 12

  • B.Des in Fashion Design (4 years) — the main route; top entry via the NIFT entrance, NID DAT, or UCEED for design programmes.
  • B.Sc / BA in Fashion or Textile Design — offered by many universities with their own tests or merit admission.
  • Diplomas in fashion / apparel design — shorter, practical routes into the industry.
  • M.Des / specialisation — for depth in textiles, luxury, or fashion management later.

Core skills to build

  • Creativity and a point of view — original ideas, not just technical neatness.
  • Sketching and visualisation — getting what’s in your head onto paper and into cloth.
  • Craft knowledge — fabrics, construction, pattern-making and finishing.
  • Business and resilience — the industry is competitive; grit matters as much as talent.

Is this career right for you?

Fashion design suits strong Artistic interests paired with Enterprising (bringing ideas to market). If you are visually driven, original and willing to pair creativity with the unglamorous craft and business of the industry, it is a genuine fit — and talent plus a portfolio matter more than your Class 12 stream.

Key takeaways
  • Any stream can enter; a strong portfolio and the NIFT/NID entrance matter most.
  • B.Des is the main degree, with design institutes and university routes alongside.
  • It is a real industry with craft, deadlines and business, not only glamour.
  • Best-fit interests: Artistic + Enterprising. You are creative and want to see ideas reach people.
