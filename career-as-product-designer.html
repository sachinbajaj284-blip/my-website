How to Become a Product Designer (UX/UI) in India

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated July 2026 · 8 min read

Product design — often called UX/UI design — is the craft of making apps, websites and products that feel effortless to use. It sits at the meeting point of creativity, psychology and technology, it is portfolio-driven, and it is one of the most stream-open careers going.

What a product designer actually does

A product designer figures out what users need and designs the experience that meets it — researching users, mapping flows, sketching wireframes, building interactive prototypes (in tools like Figma), and refining based on feedback. UX (user experience) leans toward research and structure; UI (user interface) leans toward the visual craft. Most designers blend both, working closely with engineers and product managers to ship real products.

The path after Class 10

Any stream works — choose on your broader interests. Design entrance exams reward observation, creativity and visual reasoning, not particular Class-12 subjects. If you naturally notice why one app feels easy and another frustrating, or you like both making things and understanding people, you already have the instincts this field is built on.

The path after Class 12

  • UCEED — entrance for B.Des at IITs (Bombay, Delhi, etc.) and IIITDM.
  • NID DAT — the Design Aptitude Test for the National Institute of Design.
  • NIFT — for design programmes with a fashion/lifestyle lean.
  • Other degrees + specialisation — many designers come from engineering, arts or other degrees and move in via UX courses, bootcamps and a strong portfolio.

Core skills to build

  • User research & empathy — understanding real people, not guessing.
  • Wireframing & prototyping — Figma is the industry-standard tool.
  • Interaction & visual design — layout, hierarchy, usability.
  • A portfolio — in design, your work speaks louder than any certificate.

Is this career right for you?

Product design suits Artistic interests in the Holland RIASEC model — creativity and aesthetic judgement — combined with Investigative curiosity about people and Enterprising drive to ship real work. Crucially, you do not need to be a classical illustrator; empathy, structured thinking and a good eye matter more. If you care about how things feel to use, this field will feel like home.

Key takeaways
  • Any stream works — design is portfolio- and aptitude-driven.
  • Routes: B.Des via UCEED/NID/NIFT, or another degree plus a strong portfolio.
  • Skills beat certificates: research, wireframing, Figma, a real portfolio.
  • Best-fit interests: Artistic + Investigative + Enterprising; empathy over illustration.
