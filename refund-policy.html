This policy applies to payments made for Lume Live sessions, assessment reports, digital resources, parent guidance products, career intelligence roadmaps, working-professional profile services and online psychology tuition.

1

General Refund Principle

We aim to be fair and practical. Refunds are considered when a payment is duplicated, a service cannot be delivered because of a technical issue, or Lume Live is unable to provide the paid service. Once a digital report/resource has been accessed, generated, downloaded or delivered, refunds are generally not available.

3

Duplicate or Failed Payment

If money is debited twice or payment succeeds but the website does not unlock access, contact us with the payment reference, phone number and screenshot if available. We will verify the payment and either restore access, deliver the service manually or process a refund where applicable.

4

Psychology Tuition

Psychology tuition is paid one month at a time. Each payment covers one month of classes from the date of the first class, together with the notes, PYQs, PPTs and other study material shared during that month. There is no automatic renewal or auto-debit: to continue, the student pays for the next month.

Before classes startFull refundif you cancel before the first class of the paid month. If we are unable to start classes within 7 days of your payment, you may choose a full refund.
After classes startNon-refundable for that monthonce the first class of the month has been held, as the fee covers the tutor's time and the study material already shared.
Next monthPay again, or stopyou can stop at any time simply by not paying for the next month. No notice period applies.
  • Missed classesIf the student lets us know at least 24 hours in advance, we will reschedule the class within the same month where possible. A class missed without notice is counted as taken. If a class is cancelled by Lume Live, it is always rescheduled.
  • Study materialNotes, PYQs and PPTs are for the enrolled student's personal use and may not be shared or resold.
5

Session No-Show

If a client does not attend a confirmed session without prior notice, the booking may be considered used. Lume Live may still offer a reschedule at its discretion depending on the situation.
