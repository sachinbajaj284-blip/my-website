How to Become a Dentist in India

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated October 2026 · 8 min read

Dentistry is precise, hands-on healthcare with the option of running your own practice. It shares the NEET gateway with MBBS but is a distinct, and often underrated, path for a steady, detail-oriented student.

💼 What a dentist actually does

A dentist diagnoses and treats problems of the teeth, gums and mouth — from fillings, extractions and root canals to orthodontics, implants and oral surgery. Many dentists eventually run their own clinics, which makes it part healthcare and part small business. Specialisation (MDS) opens fields like orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics and maxillofacial surgery.

🧭 The path after Class 10

Take Science with Biology (PCB) — it is required for NEET and for BDS. Chemistry and Physics matter for the entrance exam, and Biology is the foundation of everything that follows.

🎓 The path after Class 12

  • BDS — Bachelor of Dental Surgery (5 years, incl. a 1-year internship) — the single degree route, admission strictly through NEET UG.
  • MDS — Master of Dental Surgery (3 years) — specialisation after BDS, via NEET MDS; strongly recommended for better prospects.
  • Practice, hospital or academia — after BDS you can join a clinic, start your own, work in a hospital, or move into teaching and research.

🛠️ Core skills to build

  • Manual dexterity — steady, precise hands working in a very small, delicate space.
  • Spatial and clinical judgement — reading X-rays and planning treatment accurately.
  • Patient reassurance — many patients are anxious; calm, clear communication matters.
  • Business sense — if you run your own clinic, it is also an enterprise to manage.

🧩 Is this career right for you?

Dentistry suits a blend of Realistic (skilled, hands-on work), Investigative (diagnosis) and Social (patient care) interests. If you are precise with your hands, patient with detail and comfortable reassuring people, it is a strong fit — and a real alternative to MBBS for many NEET aspirants.

Key takeaways
  • BDS is the only route, and admission is strictly through NEET UG.
  • An MDS specialisation meaningfully improves prospects and earnings.
  • Pay is modest early, but private practice can grow substantially over time.
  • Best-fit interests: Realistic + Investigative + Social. You like precise, hands-on, people-facing work.
