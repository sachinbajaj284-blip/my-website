What counselling for low mood can help with

You don’t need a diagnosis or a reason that looks big enough from outside. These are the things people bring when low mood has been weighing on them:

🌁
Loss of interest

Things you used to enjoy going flat — hobbies, people, plans. Often the earliest and most honest sign.

😶
Exhaustion & sleep

Tiredness sleep doesn’t fix, mornings getting harder, everything taking more effort than it should.

🧠
The harsh inner voice

Guilt, feeling like a burden, a running self-criticism you’d never accept aimed at anyone else.

😓
Withdrawing

Pulling back from friends and family without quite deciding to, and the loneliness that follows.

🌑
Numbness, not just sadness

Feeling flat or empty rather than tearful — the version of low mood that’s easiest to miss and dismiss.

🫀
Feeling stuck

Drift after a setback, a loss or a change — where low mood and feeling directionless feed each other.

Frequently asked questions

Can counselling help with depression?

Often, yes — especially for mild to moderate low mood, where counselling, behavioural change, sleep, movement and support can make a real difference. More severe depression sometimes needs medication alongside that, and a counsellor can help you judge which and refer you on.

Is the low-mood check a diagnosis?

No. The PHQ-4 is a brief reflection scale that tells you roughly how heavy the last two weeks have been. It isn’t a diagnosis — only a qualified clinician can make one — and your answers aren’t stored or sent anywhere.

I feel numb rather than sad. Is that still worth talking about?

Yes. Loss of feeling — not enjoying anything, feeling flat or empty — is one of the core features of depression and is often missed precisely because it doesn’t look like the sadness people expect. It’s very much worth talking through.

Will anyone find out?

No. Nothing is shared with your parents, school, college or employer — no report, no summary, not even confirmation that you attended. A first name is enough to book.

What if I’m having thoughts of not wanting to be here?

Please don’t sit with that alone. Counselling isn’t a crisis service — if you’re in immediate distress, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 now. It’s free, confidential and available 24×7.
