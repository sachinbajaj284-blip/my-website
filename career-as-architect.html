How to Become an Architect in India

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated July 2026 · 8 min read

Architecture is where art meets engineering — designing buildings and spaces that are beautiful, functional and safe. It is a creative career with a technical spine, and in India it needs a specific stream and a licence, so the planning starts early.

What an architect actually does

Architects design buildings and spaces from first sketch to finished structure — balancing the client’s needs, aesthetics, budgets, safety codes and the physics of construction. The work blends creativity with coordination: drawing and 3D modelling, site visits, working with engineers and contractors, and problem-solving when the real world meets the drawing. Specialisations include urban design, landscape, interior and sustainable architecture.

The path after Class 10

Take Science with Mathematics (PCM) — Maths in Class 12 is mandatory for B.Arch admission, so this is the one firm requirement. A natural feel for design, drawing and spatial thinking is a big advantage, but it is developed as much as it is innate. If you love both making things and solving how they stand up, this stream sets you up.

The path after Class 12

  • NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture) — the primary entrance, testing aptitude, drawing and reasoning.
  • JEE Main Paper 2 (B.Arch) — an alternative/additional route, especially for NITs and some top institutes.
  • B.Arch — a 5-year degree including a practical training semester.
  • COA registration — register with the Council of Architecture to use the title “Architect” and practise.
  • M.Arch (optional) — specialise in urban design, landscape, conservation or interiors.

Architecture is a long degree (5 years) with modest early pay that grows with reputation and portfolio. It rewards patience and love of the craft — go in for the work, not the starting salary.

Is this career right for you?

Architecture suits a blend of Artistic and Realistic interests in the Holland RIASEC model — creativity paired with hands-on, spatial, build-it thinking — often with Investigative problem-solving. If you find yourself noticing how spaces are put together, enjoy both drawing and figuring out how things work, and can handle detailed, deadline-driven project work, this is a strong match.

Key takeaways
  • PCM with Maths is required for B.Arch — plan the stream after Class 10.
  • Entry is via NATA and/or JEE Main Paper 2; the degree is 5 years.
  • COA registration is needed to practise as an “Architect”.
  • Best-fit interests: Artistic + Realistic + Investigative, with patience for a long craft.
