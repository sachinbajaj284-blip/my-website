How to Become a Chef in India

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

Being a chef is craft, pressure and creativity on a plate — a hands-on career for people who love food and can thrive in a hot, fast kitchen. The glamour on TV is real; so are the long hours behind it.

💼 What a chef actually does

A chef prepares food and runs a kitchen — developing dishes and menus, managing ingredients and cost, and leading a brigade under real time pressure. Careers range from hotels and restaurants to cruise lines, airlines, cloud kitchens, food media and your own venture. The path usually climbs from commis to chef de partie, sous chef and executive chef, and hospitality training opens the wider hotel-management field too.

🧭 The path after Class 10

Stream is not a barrier — any stream can lead to the kitchen. What counts is a genuine love of food and willingness for hard, practical work. Students who are sure can start with hospitality or culinary programmes right after Class 12.

🎓 The path after Class 12

  • BHM — Bachelor of Hotel Management (3–4 years) — the broad route into hospitality and the kitchen; top entry via NCHMCT JEE into the IHMs.
  • B.Sc in Hospitality & Hotel Administration / Culinary Arts — degree routes with a stronger kitchen focus.
  • Culinary diplomas and craft courses — shorter, intensely practical routes straight into professional cooking.
  • Apprenticeship in a good kitchen — real-world training that, with skill, can matter as much as the certificate.

🛠️ Core skills to build

  • Cooking craft and palate — technique, taste and consistency, plate after plate.
  • Speed under pressure — a busy service is relentless; calm hands win.
  • Stamina and discipline — long hours on your feet, weekends and festivals included.
  • Leadership and costing — running a brigade and a kitchen’s numbers as you rise.

🧩 Is this career right for you?

The kitchen suits Realistic interests (skilled, physical, hands-on work) blended with Artistic (creativity on the plate) and, as you rise, Enterprising (leading a brigade or a business). If you love food, don’t mind heat and pressure, and take pride in craft, it is a strong fit — just go in clear-eyed about the hours.

Key takeaways
  • Any stream can enter; BHM via NCHMCT JEE and culinary programmes are the main routes.
  • The career climbs from commis to executive chef — skill and stamina drive it.
  • The work is demanding: long hours, weekends and real pressure behind the glamour.
  • Best-fit interests: Realistic + Artistic (+ Enterprising later). You love food and hands-on craft.
