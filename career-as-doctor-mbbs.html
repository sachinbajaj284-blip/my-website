How to Become a Doctor (MBBS) in India

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated July 2026 · 10 min read

Medicine is one of the longest, most demanding and most respected careers in India — and it is a genuine calling, not just a job title. The path is clear and single-gated through NEET, but it is long, and it asks for both academic strength and real emotional resilience.

What a doctor actually does

Beyond diagnosing and treating illness, doctors carry responsibility for real lives daily — long hours, difficult conversations, and continuous learning as medicine advances. The field is vast: general practice, surgery, paediatrics, psychiatry, radiology, public health and dozens of specialisations, each a different daily life. It is deeply meaningful work, and it is demanding work; both are true.

The path after Class 10

Take Science with Biology (PCB) — this is non-negotiable, because NEET requires Physics, Chemistry and Biology. Some students take PCMB (all four subjects) to keep engineering open as a backup, at the cost of a heavier workload. If you are certain about medicine, PCB with focused NEET preparation is the cleaner path.

The path after Class 12

  • NEET-UG — the single national entrance exam for MBBS and BDS across all government and private colleges. Your rank decides your college.
  • MBBS — 5.5 years including a compulsory one-year rotating internship.
  • NEET-PG → MD / MS — to specialise (~3 years), followed optionally by super-specialisation (DM/MCh).
  • Allied routes — if medicine does not work out, BDS (dentistry), BPT (physiotherapy), B.Sc Nursing, Pharmacy and biotechnology are related, respected fields.

Is this career right for you?

Medicine suits students who combine Investigative interests (science, diagnosis, evidence) with strong Social ones (a real desire to help people) in the Holland RIASEC model. Just as important is temperament: the ability to stay calm under pressure, handle long study years, and cope with the emotional weight of the work. Marks alone do not make a good doctor — and marks alone rarely sustain someone through the long road.

Key takeaways
  • PCB is required; NEET-UG is the single gate for MBBS in India.
  • MBBS is 5.5 years; specialising adds several more — plan for a long journey.
  • NEET rank drives everything — college, cost and options.
  • Best-fit interests: Investigative + Social, plus genuine resilience under pressure.
