01 Critical Peak season is now

Your only real traffic weapon was built, tested, and never switched on

Someone did excellent work here. There is a full JoSAA cutoff pipeline — an ingester, a validator, a parser test suite, and a page generator that deliberately writes cutoff tables into the HTML because "a client-rendered table indexes as an empty page." It is wired to a scheduled GitHub Action. It has never produced a single row.

The probe failure is small and specific. JoSAA changed its page; discoverFields() identifies each dropdown by its option text rather than its id, and JoSAA now serves those dropdowns empty on first load, so nothing matches. The probe already printed the live control names for you:

Note there is no gender dropdown on that page any more — the ingester is still looking for one. Meanwhile college-predictor.js:136 shows every visitor: "The JoSAA dataset has not been published to this site."

This matters more than anything else on the list because cutoff and predictor queries are the highest-volume, highest-intent student searches in India, they peak between June and August, and they attract the inbound links your domain has none of. Every other page you've written — "career counselling in Pune," "what is CUET" — is a commodity that fifty sites already own. A working JoSAA predictor is not.

Do this first Fix discoverFields() to fall back to those control names, drop the gender field, then run the workflow manually with smoke_test = true. When the smoke test parses cleanly, re-run with it off to pull 2024–2026 and commit. This is days of work at most and it is the difference between a site with a reason to exist and 98 pages of undifferentiated advice. Do it before the counselling season closes.
02 Critical

Your 22 city pages look like doorway pages to Google

I measured vocabulary overlap between every city page and the Mumbai one. The metros are 68–76% identical to each other — same structure, same claims, same FAQs, with the city name swapped.

page words overlap vs Mumbai career-counselling-in-ahmedabad 1013 0.757 career-counselling-in-guwahati 1021 0.735 career-counselling-in-bangalore 1016 0.732 career-counselling-in-kolkata 1017 0.731 career-counselling-in-pune 997 0.731 ... career-counselling-in-rohtak 1900 0.284 ← genuinely different

Google's spam policy names doorway pages explicitly: pages generated for many cities that funnel users to the same content. The best case is that Google indexes one and ignores twenty-one. The worse case is that the pattern suppresses trust in the whole domain, which would also explain why nothing else ranks either. It doesn't help that these pages mostly link to each other — the homepage links to exactly one of them.

Rohtak is the exception, and it's the honest one: nearly twice the length, genuinely distinct, and the city you actually operate in.

The fix Keep Rohtak and the Haryana cluster you can actually serve — Bhiwani, Hisar, Panipat, Sonipat, Gurugram, Faridabad, Noida. Those already score lower overlap because they contain real local detail. 301-redirect the fifteen metros you have no presence in into one strong "online career counselling in India" page. Fewer, thicker, honest pages beat twenty-two thin ones every time.
03 Critical

You are flying blind, and you cannot run ads even if you wanted to

google-site-verification 0 pages of 98 Facebook / Meta Pixel 0 pages Google Ads conversion tag 0 pages GA4 (G-1CZ93P4P3V) 96 pages ok missing: internship-confidentiality-agreement.html internship-consent-shadowing.html

GA4 tells you what happened after someone arrived. It cannot tell you whether Google has indexed you, which queries you appear for, or whether that 404ing colleges sitemap is throwing errors. That's Search Console, and there's no verification file anywhere in this repo.

The second half is more expensive. With no Meta Pixel and no Google Ads tag, you cannot run paid acquisition, and — more painfully — you cannot retarget. People take your free assessment, get a result, and leave forever. In India, Meta ads aimed at parents are the fastest paid channel in this category, and warm retargeting of assessment-takers is the cheapest conversion you will ever buy. You're throwing that audience away every day.

The fix Verify Search Console this week and submit both sitemaps (after the colleges one exists). Add the Meta Pixel and a Google Ads tag — your CSP already permits the pattern, so it's a header change and a connect-src entry. Fire a custom event when someone completes the free assessment; that's your retargeting audience.
04 Important

The homepage makes a parent on 4G wait for a payment SDK

index.html 280 KB of which 146 KB is inline JS largest inline blocks: 60 KB · 29 KB · 15 KB · 11 KB index.css 200 KB render-blocking, no defer/async: sdk.cashfree.com/js/v3/cashfree.js external cashfree-payments.js 55 KB lume-coupons.js 33 KB lume-capture.js 6 KB defer ok

The 60 KB block is a personality item bank; the 15 KB block is a clinical screener. None of it is needed to render the page someone lands on. The Cashfree SDK and the coupon engine load synchronously on a page where no payment occurs. On a mid-range Android phone on Indian 4G — which is most of your audience — that's a slow first paint, and mobile page experience is both a ranking input and the reason people bounce before your copy ever loads.

Credit where it's due: your images are handled properly, which is usually the bigger sin. This is a script problem, not an asset problem.

The fix Load the Cashfree SDK and cashfree-payments.js only when someone actually reaches checkout. Move the assessment item banks out of index.html into a file the assessment page loads. Add defer to what's left.

07 Important Revenue

The channel that actually pays in India is already built and sitting unused

You have school-partnerships.html, school-proposal.html and partner-with-us.html in this repo. That is the business. One school contract covers 200–800 students in a single signature, at a per-student rate you never have to discount, with the school doing the distribution for you. Reaching 800 students at ₹249 each, one parent at a time through Google, is a far harder path to the same money.

You have a real advantage here that the national players don't: you're physically in Rohtak, in Haryana, within reach of Delhi NCR. Mindler and iDreamCareer sell to schools over video calls from Gurugram offices. You can sit in a principal's office on a Tuesday.

The fix Treat D2C as lead generation and brand proof, not as the revenue line. Take the school proposal you already wrote to twenty Haryana and NCR schools in person before the academic year settles. Use the free assessment as the door-opener — offer it to a whole class at no cost, then sell the counselling layer on the back of the results.

What to do, in order

Sequenced so that nothing here depends on something later in the list.

Week 1

Stop the bleeding and start measuring

  1. Verify Google Search Console and submit the sitemap. You cannot manage what you can't see.
  2. Fix the JoSAA probe and run the workflow as a smoke test. Everything about your traffic ceiling depends on this.
  3. Remove sitemap-colleges.xml from robots.txt until it exists — you're currently pointing Google at a 404.
  4. Add the Meta Pixel so you start building a retargeting audience today rather than in a month.
Weeks 2–4

Ship the asset and clean up the liability

  1. Full JoSAA ingest and page generation for 2024–2026, committed and in the sitemap. This is your linkable asset.
  2. Consolidate the metro city pages into one national page with 301s; keep the Haryana and NCR cluster.
  3. Defer the payment stack and move the assessment item banks off the homepage.
  4. Raise prices and add the ₹3,499–₹4,999 middle tier before any ad spend starts.
Months 2–3

Build the two channels that compound

  1. Google Business Profile plus twenty real reviews, and listings on Sulekha and Justdial — this wins local intent faster than any content you write.
  2. Twenty school meetings using the proposal already in this repo. One signature changes your year.
  3. Meta retargeting to everyone who completed the free assessment, at the new price points.
  4. Publish the JoSAA data as a resource — cutoff analyses, branch trends, round-by-round movement. That earns the inbound links this domain has never had.

The short version

You don't have a content problem or an SEO-hygiene problem — both are in better shape than your competitors'. You have three problems: the one genuinely differentiated thing you built has no data in it and is silently erroring in front of every visitor; twenty-two lookalike city pages may be actively damaging your standing with Google; and you've never measured, promoted, or priced the business at a level where growth is possible.

Fix the JoSAA pipeline first, and fix it this month — counselling season is running right now and it will not wait for the rest of the list.
