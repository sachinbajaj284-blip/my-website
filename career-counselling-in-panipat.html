Career counselling in Panipat — for the city where "business join kar lega" is the default plan

Panipat is a city of enterprise — textiles, exports, trading — and that shapes how career conversations happen at home. For many Panipat students, the unspoken default is the family business; for others, the pressure runs the opposite way, toward a "safe" government or engineering path far from the family trade. Both defaults share the same flaw: they're decided by circumstance, not by who the student actually is.

Career counselling in Panipat, done properly, doesn't argue for or against the family business. It puts real data on the table — the student's tested interests, work values and learning style — so the family can see whether the default path genuinely fits, needs adjusting, or deserves an honest rethink. Sachin Bajaj (M.Sc Clinical Psychology, Gurugram University; PGDGC, Jamia Millia Islamia) conducts every session personally, online, from his base in Rohtak.

What a session covers for Panipat students

  • Stream selection after Class 10 — including the honest question Panipat families rarely ask out loud: is Commerce being chosen for the student, or for the shop?
  • After Class 12 — degree choice, entrance exams, and whether a business-adjacent path (management, design, supply chain, finance) fits better than a pure default either way.
  • Family business vs own path — a neutral, structured conversation where both the student's tested profile and the family's practical realities get equal weight.
  • Exam stress and anxiety — handled by the same counsellor, because for students the career pressure and the emotional pressure are usually the same thing. See our mental health counselling page.
