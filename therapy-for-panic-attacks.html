What counselling for panic attacks can help with

You don’t need a diagnosis to reach out. These are the things people bring when panic has taken hold:

💥
Sudden surges of fear

Intense fear that arrives out of nowhere, peaks fast, and feels completely overwhelming while it lasts.

💓
The physical storm

Pounding heart, breathlessness, dizziness, chest tightness, tingling — symptoms that mimic a medical emergency.

😰
Fear of the next one

The dread between attacks — watching your body for signs — that quietly keeps the whole cycle running.

🚫
Avoidance

Steering clear of places or situations where an attack once happened, with your world slowly narrowing.

😮‍💨
The “am I dying?” thought

The terror that this one is a heart attack or that you’ll lose control — frightening, and very addressable.

🌙
Night-time panic

Waking suddenly in a state of panic, and the sleep problems that build around it.

Frequently asked questions

Can counselling actually stop my panic attacks?

For most people, yes — panic responds very well to the right support. Counselling helps you understand what’s happening in your body, stop fighting the sensations in the way that fuels them, and gently reverse the avoidance. The frequency and intensity usually come down a great deal.

Are panic attacks dangerous?

They feel dangerous but are not physically harmful in themselves — they’re a surge of the body’s alarm response. That said, if you’re ever unsure whether symptoms are panic or something medical, get it checked; and a doctor can rule out physical causes, which is itself reassuring.

Is the anxiety check a diagnosis?

No. The GAD-7 is a brief reflection scale, not a diagnosis, and your answers aren’t stored. Only a qualified professional can diagnose a panic or anxiety disorder; if that’s the direction, your counsellor will tell you honestly.

Will anyone find out?

No. Nothing is shared with your parents, school, college or employer — no report, no summary, not even confirmation that you attended. A first name is enough to book.

What if I’m in the middle of a crisis?

Counselling isn’t a crisis service. If you’re in immediate distress, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 — free, confidential and available 24×7.
