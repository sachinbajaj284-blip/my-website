How you're graded

A certificate you can fail is worth having

Attendance alone does not earn the certificate. You pass a live practical, marked against this rubric — published here so you know exactly what you're being held to before you pay.

The practical exam

Administer and score a real protocol, unassisted25 marks
Conduct a structured intake and document the MSE20 marks
Run a 20-minute debrief with the supervisor playing the parent30 marks
Submit a client-ready report against the writing rubric25 marks
Pass mark: 60. Below 60 you may resit once, free, with the next cohort. Your grade is printed on your certificate — a distinction should mean something to whoever reads it.

What we will not promise you

Paid psychology internships in India have earned a bad reputation, and much of it is deserved. Here is exactly what this programme does not do — so you can decide honestly whether it's worth your money.

  • We will not guarantee you a Letter of Recommendation. It goes to top performers on merit. A recommendation everyone receives is worth nothing to the person reading it.
  • We will not give you real clients to counsel. You are a trainee. You shadow with consent and practise on peers under supervision — anyone offering you unsupervised clients is putting both of you at risk. Our Ethics & Consent Framework sets out exactly where the line sits.
  • We will not make you a licensed psychologist. This complements your degree. It does not replace a formal qualification, RCI registration, or statutory supervised hours.
  • We will not guarantee you a job. The pre-placement interview is real and so is the paid pool, but it is offered on merit after assessment, never at enrolment.
  • We will not accept you just because you paid. If the screening call says this isn't right for you, we refund you rather than take the money.

If what you need is a certificate for your CV without doing the work, buy a cheaper one — they are easy to find. Ours takes 60 hours, is graded, and you can fail it. That is the entire point.
