The method

We teach psychology using psychology

Each part of the course is built on a learning principle from the syllabus itself, so you remember more and cram less.

Spaced repetition

Revision that beats the forgetting curve

Chapters come back on a planned revision schedule, not the night before, so they stay in long-term memory.

Retrieval practice

PYQs with model answers

CBSE previous year questions sorted by chapter, answered the way examiners mark them.

Chunking

Chapter-wise notes

Typed and handwritten notes that break each chapter into small pieces you can revise in minutes.

Dual coding

PPTs and diagrams

Words and visuals together. Every theory and diagram from class is on a slide you can come back to.

Scaffolding

Live 1:1 or small batch

Support that fits the student: more help where a concept is new, less as it clicks. Zone of proximal development, applied.

Positive reinforcement

Tests with real feedback

Do you teach psychology tuition online for students outside my city?

Yes. All classes are live and online, so a student anywhere in India, whether in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Jaipur, Lucknow, Rohtak or a small town, gets the same teacher and the same material. You do not need to find a psychology teacher near you.

Who teaches the psychology classes?

Classes are led by a psychology teacher with 6 years of experience teaching Psychology in CBSE schools, supported by the Lume Live team of psychology tutors. Everyone on the team teaches from the NCERT textbook and the current CBSE syllabus.

Which classes and boards do you cover?

Our focus is CBSE Class 11 and Class 12 Psychology, which follows the NCERT textbooks. Because most state boards and ISC share much of the same content, students from other boards are welcome too. Tell us your board when you enrol.

What study material do I get?

Chapter-wise notes (typed and handwritten), previous year questions (PYQs) with model answers, PPTs used in class, sample papers and revision sheets, plus guidance for the Class 12 practical file and case profile. You can download free sample notes for Class 11 and Class 12 Chapter 1 on this page.

The fee is ₹7,999 per month for Class 11 or Class 12 Psychology. It covers live online classes and all the study material: chapter-wise notes, PYQs with model answers, PPTs, sample papers and practical file guidance. There is no separate charge for notes.

Yes. You can choose one-to-one tuition or a small batch. One-to-one suits students who need to catch up or want focused board exam preparation; small batches suit steady, chapter-by-chapter learning. Message us on WhatsApp for current timings.

The CUET Psychology domain paper is based on the NCERT Class 12 Psychology syllabus, so strong board preparation covers most of it. We can add MCQ practice for students who are also taking CUET.
