How it works

  • Your client pays Lume Live ₹999 for the Full Clarity Report. You pay nothing per client.
  • You earn ₹300 per report — 30% of what the client paid, up to ₹300. If they used a discount code, it's 30% of the lower price.
  • We pay monthly, by UPI, once a report is 7 days old.
  • Your sessions are yours. Charge what you like; your clients pay you directly.
  • A client stays yours. Once a client buys through your link, their later reports count for you too — and another counsellor's link doesn't change that.
  • Your clients don't see Lume Live's own session offers when they arrive on your link.
  • Buying a report for yourself on your own link doesn't earn commission.
