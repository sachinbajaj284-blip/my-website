What is career counselling — and why Rohtak families are searching for it

Career counselling in Rohtak has quietly changed over the last few years. It used to mean a relative's opinion, a coaching-centre brochure, or a "DMIT test" camp at a local school. Today, more Rohtak parents and students are looking for something more structured: a real conversation with a qualified counsellor, backed by validated psychometric tools, that helps separate genuine interest from family pressure or neighbourhood comparison.

At its core, career counselling is a guided process — not a verdict. A trained counsellor helps a student reflect on their interests, aptitude, personality and values, and then connects that self-understanding to realistic stream, course and career options. It does not "assign" a career. It gives you evidence and a structured conversation so the final decision is the student's own, made with clarity instead of confusion.
