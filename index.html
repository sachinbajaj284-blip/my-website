Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our counselling process, sessions, and how we can help you or your child.

What is career counselling and who needs it?
Career counselling is a structured process that helps individuals consider interests, preferences, opportunities and practical constraints when making career decisions. It can support students choosing streams, college students considering next steps and working professionals exploring change. Lume Live combines guided exploration with 1:1 counselling; any formal assessment is separately explained before use.
How does online counselling work? Is it as effective as in-person?
Yes, online counselling is highly effective. All sessions are conducted via secure video calls (Google Meet or Zoom) from the comfort of your home. Research shows that tele-counselling delivers outcomes comparable to in-person sessions. We have successfully guided 500+ students and professionals across India through online sessions.
What happens in the first ₹249 session?
Your first session is a 45-minute 1:1 conversation with your Lume Live counsellor at ₹249 (regular ₹499). We begin by understanding your current concerns, whether they relate to stream choices, career direction or emotional stress. Any questionnaire or separately authorised assessment used in counselling is explained with its purpose and limitations first.
Is everything I share kept confidential?
Absolutely. 100% confidentiality is our highest priority. Everything shared during sessions — personal struggles, family issues, academic concerns — stays strictly between you and your counsellor. We follow professional ethical guidelines and do not share any information with parents, schools, or third parties without your explicit consent.
Can parents attend the session with their child?
Yes, we offer joint parent-child sessions upon request. These are especially helpful when there is disagreement about stream or career choices. Our approach ensures the student's voice is heard while addressing parent concerns through structured, respectful discussion. We also offer separate parent guidance sessions.
How many sessions will I need?
Most students achieve significant clarity in 2–3 sessions. The first session focuses on assessment and immediate guidance. Follow-up sessions deepen the roadmap, address emotional blocks, and refine action plans. For emotional wellness concerns, the number varies based on individual needs — we never push unnecessary sessions.
Do you conduct sessions for schools and groups?
Yes. We conduct career-awareness workshops, mental-health seminars and structured guidance activities for schools across India. Any formal assessment programme is discussed separately with the school, including purpose, consent and reporting arrangements.
