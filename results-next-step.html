Frequently asked questions

I just got my result and feel lost. Where do I start?

Start with the free 60-second Career Snapshot — it needs no payment and gives you a first read on which direction fits you. Then pick your situation above for the honest options, and if you want a person to think it through with, a first 1:1 session with a qualified counsellor is ₹499.

My child scored lower than expected. What are the real options?

A lower score closes fewer doors than it feels like in the first week. Depending on the exam there are strong alternative courses, private and state counselling rounds, and genuinely good paths outside the one that was planned. A structured session separates a passing setback from a real change of plan and maps the realistic options calmly.

Is a drop year worth it for NEET or JEE?

Sometimes yes, often no — and it should be decided on evidence, not momentum. We look at aptitude, the realistic gain from another attempt, the student’s own motivation and the strong alternatives, so the choice to repeat or move on is made deliberately. Our guide for droppers covers this in depth.

How soon should we decide on a stream or course?

Sooner is calmer, but rushed is worse than considered. The first few days after a result are for steadying down, not for locking in a decision under pressure. A free snapshot now and one structured conversation within a week or two is usually the right pace.

Can we do this in Hindi?

Yes. Sessions run in Hindi or English, whichever the family thinks in, over secure video call so everyone can join from home anywhere in India.
