5 quiet signs of exam burnout

Burnout rarely announces itself. Here's what to watch for — and what helps. Tap through →
LUME LIVE
Sign 1

Studying more, absorbing less

Hours go up, but nothing sticks. Re-reading the same page without it registering is a classic early warning.
LUME LIVE
Sign 2

Sleep and appetite change

Trouble falling asleep, waking exhausted, or eating far more or less than usual. The body signals before the mind admits it.
LUME LIVE
Sign 3

Irritability and withdrawal

Snapping at family, dropping friends and hobbies, going quiet. Burnout often looks like a personality change.
LUME LIVE
Sign 4

Blanking out under pressure

Knowing the material but freezing in the test. When anxiety hijacks memory, it's a sign the load has become too heavy.
LUME LIVE
Sign 5

“What's the point?”

Persistent hopelessness or dread about exams and the future. This one matters most — please don't wait it out alone.
What helps

Small steps work: regular sleep, short breaks, talking to someone, and evidence-based techniques for exam anxiety. If it's persistent, reach out — support helps.
