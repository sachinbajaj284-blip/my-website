How an online session works

  1. Start with the free Career Snapshot. Sixty seconds, no payment, no sign-up. It gives the counsellor a first read of the student's interests. Take it here.
  2. Book a slot. On WhatsApp or the booking page, Monday to Saturday, 9am to 8pm IST.

Does it work if you're not in Haryana?

Yes. Lume Live is based in Rohtak and every session is online, so it makes no difference whether you're in Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai, Jaipur, Lucknow or a smaller town. You get the same counsellor, the same assessment and the same price. We don't have offices in other cities and we won't pretend to.

What does differ from state to state is the route into college, and we plan around yours:

  • Your board. CBSE, ICSE or your state board each change how marks, subject combinations and board-exam pressure play out.
  • Your state's entrance exams. Alongside JEE, NEET and CUET, many states run their own: MHT CET in Maharashtra, KCET in Karnataka, WBJEE in West Bengal, TS EAPCET in Telangana, KEAM in Kerala, GUJCET in Gujarat. A state seat is often the most realistic good option, so it belongs in the plan.
  • Home-state quota and fees. Domicile seats and fee differences can change which college is actually the best choice for your family.
One honest limit: sessions are in Hindi or English. If the student is only comfortable in another language, we're not the right fit yet.

Validated assessment, never DMIT

Fingerprint-based "DMIT" career tests have no standing in mainstream psychology and cannot measure interests or aptitude. We use validated frameworks: Holland RIASEC for career interests, VARK for learning style, and a Career Values inventory, each explained in plain language before use. Why we don't offer DMIT.

Frequently asked questions

Is online career counselling as good as meeting in person?

For career counselling, yes. What helps is a validated psychometric assessment followed by a careful 1:1 conversation, and both work fully over a video call. Online also makes it easier for both parents to join, which matters because most stream and college decisions are family decisions.

I live in Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata or another city far from Haryana. Can I still book?

Yes. Every session is online, so the counsellor, the assessment and the price are the same wherever you live. Lume Live is based in Rohtak, Haryana and has no offices in other cities; we say that plainly rather than pretending to be local.

Which languages are sessions in?

Hindi and English, or a mix of both. We do not currently offer sessions in other regional languages, so please check that one of these works comfortably for the student before booking.

How much does online career counselling cost?

The first 45-minute 1:1 session is ₹499. The 60-second Career Snapshot is free, and the optional ₹999 Full Clarity Report adds a detailed psychometric profile. There are no compulsory packages.

Do you know my state board and my state's entrance exams?

We work with CBSE, ICSE and state-board students, and we look at your state's own entrance route alongside the national ones: for example MHT CET in Maharashtra, KCET in Karnataka, WBJEE in West Bengal, TS EAPCET in Telangana, KEAM in Kerala or GUJCET in Gujarat. We check current dates and rules with you in the session, because these change from year to year.
