How to Become a Chartered Accountant in India

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated July 2026 · 9 min read

Chartered Accountancy is one of India’s most respected professional qualifications — a clear, exam-defined path with no admission lottery. It rewards discipline and consistency over a few years, and opens careers in audit, tax, finance, consulting and business leadership.

What a Chartered Accountant actually does

CAs are the financial backbone of businesses. They audit accounts, plan and file taxes, ensure companies follow financial law, advise on investments and mergers, and increasingly sit in senior strategy and CFO roles. The work can be in practice (your own firm or a Big-4 style firm), in industry (finance teams of companies), or in consulting. It is precise, responsibility-heavy work where accuracy genuinely matters.

The path after Class 10

Commerce is the natural stream — it front-loads the accounting and economics you will study for years. But ICAI accepts students from any stream, and Science and Arts students do become CAs. Whatever you pick, keeping Mathematics in Class 11–12 makes the quantitative papers considerably easier.

The path after Class 12 (the CA route)

  1. CA Foundation — register with ICAI after Class 12 and clear the entry-level exam.
  2. CA Intermediate — the middle stage, covering accounting, law, costing, taxation and more.
  3. Articleship — roughly two to three years of supervised practical training with a practising CA. This is where the profession is really learned.
  4. CA Final — the last set of exams; clearing them earns you the CA designation.
There is also a direct-entry route for graduates (commerce graduates with the required marks can skip Foundation and start at Intermediate). For most Class 12 students, though, the Foundation route is the standard path.

How long it takes & what it costs

A focused student who clears each stage without long gaps typically qualifies in around 4.5 to 5 years after Class 12. The official ICAI fees are modest compared to most professional degrees; the real cost is time, discipline and coaching. It is a marathon, not a sprint — and that is precisely why it filters for consistency.

Is this career right for you?

CA suits students whose interests lean Conventional and Enterprising in the Holland RIASEC model — people who like structure, accuracy and rules, and who are comfortable with responsibility and business. Crucially, it rewards temperament: steady, self-driven study over years beats brilliance-in-bursts. If you can work consistently toward a distant goal, CA is one of the surest routes in India.

Key takeaways
  • Commerce makes it easier, but any stream can pursue CA through ICAI.
  • The route is Foundation → Intermediate → Articleship → Final, taking ~4.5–5 years.
  • No admission lottery — discipline and consistency decide the outcome.
  • Best-fit interests: Conventional + Enterprising. You value structure, accuracy and responsibility.
