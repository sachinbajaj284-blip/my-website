What is CUET? A Simple Guide for Students & Parents

By the Lume Live counselling team · Updated July 2026 · 7 min read

CUET quietly changed how lakhs of students enter university in India — yet many families are still unsure what it is or how to approach it. This is the plain-language version: what CUET is, who it's for, and how to prepare sensibly alongside your boards.

What CUET actually is

CUET — the Common University Entrance Test — is a single national entrance exam for undergraduate admission at a large number of central, state and private universities. Instead of chasing separate, sky-high cut-offs at each university, students take one common test and use the score to apply to many participating institutions. CUET-UG is the undergraduate version most Class 12 students care about.

Who should take CUET

Any Class 12 student aiming for a UG seat at a participating university — Delhi University, BHU, Jamia Millia Islamia, Allahabad, and many more. Because it offers domain subjects across the sciences, commerce and humanities, it's relevant whatever your stream. If a university on your list accepts CUET, it's on your radar.

What's in the exam

  • Language papers — comprehension and language skills.
  • Domain subjects — e.g. Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Accountancy, History, Political Science — you pick the ones your target courses need.
  • General test — general awareness, reasoning and basic aptitude.
Choose your CUET subjects to match the courses and universities you're targeting — not at random. Working backward from your desired course is the smart way to pick papers.

CUET vs board exams

They are not the same and don't replace each other. Your board exams qualify you and set eligibility; CUET is the admission test used by participating universities. The practical takeaway: prepare for CUET alongside your boards, since the syllabi overlap but the exam styles differ.

Key takeaways
  • CUET-UG is one common test for UG admission at many universities.
  • It's relevant across all streams via its domain subjects.
  • Pick your CUET subjects to match your target courses.
  • It complements board exams — both matter; prepare together.
